## What is StableCorp?

StableCorp is an all-in-one platform that lets global founders incorporate a US entity, open compliant banking, invoice in stablecoins, and stay compliant — all from a single dashboard.

We built it because the world changed but business infrastructure didn't. Founders are earning onchain through DAOs, DeFi protocols, freelance smart contracts, and stablecoin payments — but the offchain world hasn't caught up. Banks reject you. Compliance is a maze. You're invoicing in USDC but can't pay rent.

StableCorp bridges that gap.

## What We Do

- **US Incorporation** — Delaware C-Corp or Wyoming LLC, formed in 48 hours. Registered agent, EIN, and filings handled.
- **Digital Banking** — Compliant global accounts with ACH, wire transfers, and modern payment infrastructure.
- **Stablecoin Invoicing** — Generate invoices in USDC, EURC, or USDT. Accept stablecoin or fiat. Instant settlement.
- **Automated Compliance** — KYC/AML, tax reporting, regulatory monitoring, and automated filings for both your US entity and home country.
- **FX Conversion** — Built-in USD/EUR foreign exchange.
- **Payment Links** — Shareable links for instant stablecoin payments.
- **Client & Invoice Management** — Full dashboard for managing clients, tracking invoices, and sending payments.

## Tech Stack

- **Frontend**: Next.js, React, TypeScript, Tailwind CSS
- **Backend**: Convex (real-time database + serverless functions)
- **Payments**: Bridge (banking rails), Circle (USDC/EURC)
- **Auth**: Convex Auth
- **Wallet**: RainbowKit + wagmi
- **CMS**: Sanity
